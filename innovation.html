Innovation & Leadership

For me, AI has always been less about models and more about the leap from an idea to something real in someone's hands.

That thread runs through everything I've done — a research lab in Hanoi, a startup sprint in Rome, co-founding companies out of Oxford, and now leading AI and innovation at Pixta while helping the next builders find their footing. This is that journey: what I've built, how I led it, and the people and ideas I've tried to lift along the way.

The journey — building & leading innovation

  • Where it started — R&D at Panasonic 2015 – 2018 · Vietnam

    Turning research into something a person could actually talk to.

    Opportunity Panasonic needed on-device conversational and vision AI for consumer hardware, robust enough for real homes.
    Initiative Built the core stack — face recognition (ArcFace, 3D anti-spoofing), end-to-end ASR, speaker ID and noise-robust wake-word models for smart speakers.
    My role Technical leader & AI engineer — led the R&D and turned research into working prototypes.
    Result Piloted at a healthcare center for elderly residents in Kyoto, Japan — my first taste of taking AI from lab to real users.
  • A sprint in Rome — Pi School 2018 · Rome, Italy

    A few intense weeks that taught me how fast a good idea can become a product.

    Opportunity Sorgenia, an Italian energy utility, wanted to modernise its customer experience with conversational AI.
    Initiative In an intensive international program, built Italy's first energy-sector voice bot and competed in the Notartel AI Hackathon.
    My role AI scholar — solved the business problem end-to-end in a multicultural team on a tight timeline.
    Result Co-runner-up at the hackathon; the Sorgenia bot was piloted and featured in Italian media.
  • Betting on real problems — co-founding startups 2023 – now · Oxford, UK

    Choosing problems worth solving — and taking them all the way to real customers.

    Opportunity UK regulated venues must enforce Challenge-25 and gambling self-exclusion — a real compliance pain with no good automated answer; separately, teams keep losing the knowledge inside their meetings.
    Initiative Co-founded ClientScan (biometric age verification) and TakeNote.ai (voice AI for meetings), taking each from a customer need to a shipped product with Oxford University advisers.
    My role Co-founder & AI architect — chose the problem, owned the AI architecture and model development, and worked directly with early customers.
    Result ClientScan deployed across UK hospitality & regulated gaming (>99.4% accuracy); TakeNote.ai live as a product. Architecture & evaluation detail on Projects.
  • Now — leading AI & innovation at Pixta 2019 – now · Vietnam / Japan

    Making innovation a habit for a whole product — not a one-off.

    Opportunity A stock-media platform in a competitive niche has to adopt AI across the product to keep winning on cost, quality and discovery.
    Initiative As Head of AI & Program Manager for PixtaStock, I drive experimentation and decide which ideas are worth shipping — automated quality review, learning-to-rank search, LLM assistants, generative imaging.
    My role Own the technical direction and priorities, help the team turn ideas into production systems, and coordinate with product and the Japan HQ.
    Result Several systems live in production (Auto Review at 30K images/day; learning-to-rank at +5% conversion). Current work on agentic and efficient AI is ongoing.
