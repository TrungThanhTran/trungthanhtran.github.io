  • Pixta Vietnam 2023–2026
    Auto Review — AI-Powered Image Quality Assessment
    Context Manual image QA across 20+ quality standards couldn't keep up with catalog growth on PixtaStock.
    Architecture Lightweight CV models behind an automated review pipeline on the cloud, scoring up to 30,000 images/day against every standard before human escalation.
    Decision Chose lightweight CV models over a heavy VLM: at 30K images/day the small accuracy gain didn't justify the extra cost and latency, so I optimised for throughput and kept humans in the loop for edge cases.
    Role Chief architect — set the research direction, designed the model architecture & evaluation, and led the rollout into production.
    Impact ~70% lower review cost vs. the fully manual baseline, at 99.5% precision on the model's auto-review decisions; now the standing review pipeline in production at Pixta.
    Upload · 30K/day→ Lightweight CV models→ 20+ standard checks→ Pass / human escalation
    Computer Vision Deep Learning Lightweight Models Cloud
  • ClientScan · Research 2026
    FaceAge — Scalable Facial Age Estimation
    Context Estimate age accurately from a single face crop — no body context, using public training data only.
    Architecture DINOv3-ViT-L/16 backbone with CORAL ordinal regression and four-phase progressive fine-tuning; exported to ONNX for deployment.
    Decision Face-only over body-aware (MiVOLO) to stay deployable & privacy-safe from a single crop — and still beat it on accuracy.
    Role Sole author — dataset, model, benchmark and open-source release.
    Impact MAE 3.58 yr on LAGENDA 84k, beating body-aware MiVOLO v2 (3.86); ranks #1 among non-LLM methods on the eight-dataset FaceAge benchmark.
    Face crop→ DINOv3-ViT-L/16→ CORAL ordinal head→ ONNX serving
    Foundation Models DINOv3 Ordinal Regression Age Estimation ONNX
